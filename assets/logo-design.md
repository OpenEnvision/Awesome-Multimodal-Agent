# Multimodal Agents logo

## Final asset

`logo-agent.png` is the transparent PNG used by the repository README. The symbol combines an A-shaped outline, a perceptual center, and a red action arrow. The previous `logo.png` and `logo-source.png` remain available as earlier versions.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, then refined with a second image edit. No external API key or image-editing script was used.

## Initial prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: final production logo icon for the README of "Awesome Multimodal Agents", a research collection in OpenEnvision Library.
Create one outstanding, original standalone symbol, not a sheet of options or a brand presentation. The existing README has a large origami M that feels like modeling rather than agency; replace that visual idea entirely.
Art direction: precise, distinctive, minimal contemporary research identity. A compact abstract "agent loop": two or three bold paths of perception converge into a purposeful directional gesture, and a returning curve visibly closes the feedback loop. Subtly suggest an A through the negative space if it works naturally. Treat this as one unified emblem with a compelling silhouette, not a flowchart of disconnected icons. A subtle aperture/eye-like central negative space can suggest perception, while a single crisp directional end suggests action. Strong simple geometry, optical balance, deliberate gaps, consistent broad stroke weight, maximum three large interlocking shapes. It should remain identifiable at 32 pixels and feel premium at 200 pixels.
Palette: near-black #171717 and one restrained vermilion accent #ce352c, compatible with OpenEnvision's restrained editorial design. Flat solid fills with immaculate antialiased edges. No blue, gold, gradients, shadows, bevels, 3D, textures, lettering, watermark, tiny modality glyphs, brain motif, robot head, sparkle, or surrounding box.
Composition: square 1024 by 1024 image, centered mark occupying approximately 76 percent of canvas width and height with equal clear margins. Genuinely transparent alpha background, clean transparent negative spaces; do not simulate transparency with a checkerboard or paint white holes. No words, captions, mockup, background scene, or border. Deliver a single polished brand asset.

Transparent RGBA background and genuinely transparent negative spaces, no black or white rectangle, no checkerboard. No text or additions. This is a restrained flat two-color brand icon for Awesome Multimodal Agents in OpenEnvision Library, legible at 32 px, elegant at 200 px. Output just the single refined logo.
